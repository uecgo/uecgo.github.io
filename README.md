# UEC Go App
UEC Go is a mobile application designed for UEC students in Malaysia to access free quality notes, quizzes and exercises for 10+ subjects.
This website is created for application information and support purposes.

## About
UEC Go is the ultimate study companion created for UEC students, offering organised, high-quality notes across 10+ subjects - all in
one app.

As a Chinese independent high school student, we know the challenge of juggling more than 10 subjects while preparing for the UEC exam. That's why we built UEC Go - to help you "Learn smarter, prep better, stress less".

Key Features:
﻿﻿Access notes for 10+ UEC subjects wherever, whenever
Sharpen Your Skills with Interactive Quizzes

Why UEC Go?
Unlike general study note apps, UEC Go is tailored specifically to the United Examination Certificate (UEC) syllabus and learning journey. Whether you're revising for exams or looking for peer-contributed materials, this app is designed to keep you organised, efficient, and motivated.

UEC Go helps you prepare with confidence, connect with the community, and study with purpose.
